About the Event

What is EUREKA?

EUREKA is LTCE's flagship annual tech competition — a platform where student teams transform bold ideas into working solutions and pitch them to a panel of industry experts.

Whether you're building a web app, an AI model, an IoT device, or any tech-driven innovation, EUREKA is the stage where your ideas get the spotlight they deserve.

Compete in three rounds designed to test your ideation, execution, and presentation skills — and walk away with prizes, recognition, and connections that last a lifetime.

Guidelines

Rules & Regulations

✦Participants can pitch solo or in teams of up to 7
✦Teams must consist of 1 to 7 members
✦Cross-college teams are allowed
✦All projects must be original and not submitted elsewhere
✦Plagiarism results in immediate disqualification
✦Abstract submission is mandatory before the deadline
✦A working prototype/demo is required for Round 2
✦Teams must present in person on the event day
✦Teams must present their idea through a PPT presentation during judging
✦Late registrations will not be accepted under any circumstance
✦Judges' decision is final and binding
✦A working prototype/demo is not mandatory for Round 1
📌 For rule clarifications or special cases, contact the organizing committee at eureka@ltce.ac.in
